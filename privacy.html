Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

FactRush Daily publishes short educational videos and operates an internal automation tool to upload videos to its own YouTube channel.

Information we collect

This website does not ask visitors to create accounts, submit forms, or provide personal information. We do not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on this site. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

Google and YouTube access

Our internal uploader uses Google OAuth authorization to access the FactRush Daily YouTube channel. It requests YouTube upload and read-only channel permissions for the purpose of verifying the connected channel and uploading videos. OAuth credentials are used for channel automation and are not sold or shared with advertisers.

Hosting and external links

This website may be hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses in accordance with its own policies. Links to YouTube and other external sites are governed by those sites' privacy practices.

Data retention and choices

Emails may be retained to respond to inquiries. The Google account owner can revoke the automation's access through Google Account settings. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent directly to us.
