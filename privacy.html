Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

FastFacts365 publishes short educational videos on YouTube, its Facebook Page, and its Instagram account. A private, owner-operated automation tool uploads this content through YouTube and Meta publishing APIs.

Information we collect

This website does not ask visitors to create accounts, submit forms, or provide personal information. We do not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on this site. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

Connected channel access

Our internal uploader uses Google OAuth to access the connected YouTube channel and a Meta Page access token to publish content to the owner-controlled Facebook Page and connected Instagram professional account. These credentials are used to publish educational videos and verify posting status. We do not sell access tokens or platform information to advertisers.

Hosting and external links

This website may be hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses in accordance with its own policies. Links to YouTube and other external sites are governed by those sites' privacy practices.

Data retention and choices

Emails may be retained to respond to inquiries. The Google account owner can revoke the automation's access through Google Account settings; access to Meta services can be revoked through Meta account and business integrations settings. To request deletion of information you sent directly to us or information maintained by our automation, contact us at the email below. We will evaluate and respond to requests consistent with applicable law and platform rules.
